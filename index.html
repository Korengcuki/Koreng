Capabilities

Everything a modern team needs to think faster.

Synaptic is built from six systems that were designed to work as one, not six separate products bolted together. The idea is simple: knowledge and work should be able to move through your organization without getting stuck in silos, waiting on a handoff, or living in someone's inbox.

AI Agents

Deploy agents that can actually carry a task from start to finish instead of just answering one question at a time. They read the context around a request, figure out the right sequence of steps, and take action on your behalf across whatever tools your team already relies on, checking in with a person only when it genuinely matters.

Workflow Automation

Sketch out branching, event-driven workflows on a visual canvas the same way you'd draw them on a whiteboard. Connect the systems your team depends on, set the triggers that should kick things off, and let the repetitive parts of the process run quietly on their own from then on.

Knowledge Hub

Every document, decision, and conversation your organization produces gets folded into one living, searchable index. Instead of digging through old threads or asking around to find out what was decided, anyone on the team can just search and get the current answer, not a stale one from six months ago.

Team Collaboration

Shared workspaces, live annotations, and role-based context mean people and agents are always looking at the same version of the truth. No more guessing whether you're looking at the latest file or a copy someone forgot to update three revisions ago.

Analytics Dashboard

Live dashboards give decision-makers a clear read on how agents are performing, where workflows are moving quickly or getting stuck, and how much the knowledge hub is actually being used day to day, so improvements are based on real numbers rather than a hunch.

API Integrations

Typed, versioned APIs and a growing library of native integrations mean Synaptic can slot into the stack you already have in a matter of minutes, rather than forcing your team to rip out tools that are already working fine for them.

About Synaptic

Intelligence should connect, not fragment, your team.

Most teams end up running on a patchwork of tools that were never designed to talk to each other. Chat lives in one app, documents live in another, and whatever automation exists is usually a handful of scripts someone wrote on a weekend and nobody else fully understands. Synaptic started from a simple premise: intelligence works best when it can move freely between people, agents, and systems, instead of getting trapped inside whichever tool happened to generate it.

We're a small team of engineers and researchers, and honestly we got tired of watching good ideas get lost because they lived in the wrong Slack channel. We're building the connective layer for how modern organizations think, decide, and act, and right now we're doing that in early access with a small group of teams who are helping us shape it before it opens up more widely.
